UK Castles

A map of castles across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, for finding them, ticking off the ones you have visited, and remembering your visits. It works without an internet connection.

Coming soon to Google Play.

What it does

  • 3,505 castles on one map, from great fortresses to mottes in fields, 1,651 of them well known, each with what survives today: 1,415 have standing buildings or ruins, all checked by hand.
  • Two maps: a modern one, and a medieval one in parchment and ink, with abbeys, cathedrals and the old names of places from the Domesday Book.
  • Visiting: who runs each castle and a link to its tickets and opening times, where there is one (532 castles), and which castles are not open to the public.
  • Your visits: tick castles off, rate them in ten categories, write notes and add up to three photos each. Find the nearest castles you haven't been to.
  • Back up your visits and photos to one file, and restore them on a new phone.

Private by design

No account, no adverts, no tracking. Everything you record stays on your phone, and the app cannot use the internet at all: links you tap open in your phone's browser. Read the privacy policy.

Built on open data

The castle list and maps come from OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and Wikipedia. In return, the app's castle data is free for anyone to download and reuse.
