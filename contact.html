Contact

Questions, corrections and problems are all welcome.

Open the contact form

Messages go privately to the maker of the app. The form is run by Google Forms, which passes your message on; see its privacy policy.

Something wrong with a castle?

If a castle's details are wrong (what remains, who runs it, a ticket link that no longer works or leads somewhere it shouldn't), please send a message with the castle's name and what you found. Ticket links are checked regularly, but websites change.

Reporting a security problem

If you think you have found a security problem in the app or this website, please send a message with the details, starting with the word "Security". You will get a reply, and the problem will be looked at promptly. Please don't share it publicly until it has been fixed. The same contact is in this site's security.txt.

Not affiliated

UK Castles is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, English Heritage, the National Trust, Cadw, Historic Environment Scotland, the National Trust for Scotland, Historic Houses or any other organisation named in it. Opening times, prices and access change: check the official website before you travel.
