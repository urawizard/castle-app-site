Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

In short: the UK Castles app collects no personal data. It has no account, no adverts and no tracking, and it cannot use the internet. Everything you record stays on your phone.

Who we are

UK Castles is made by Craig, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: through the contact form.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything below is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We never receive it.

WhatWhy
The castles you mark as visited, the dates, your ratings and notesTo keep your record of visits.
Photos you add (a copy of at most 2,000 pixels, with location data removed)To show with your visit. Photos you take with the camera are also saved to your phone's gallery, as with any camera app.
Your settings (map style, filters, when you last made a backup)To remember your choices.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap the location button or sort castles by distance. It is used on your phone to show where you are and which castles are nearest, and it is not stored or sent anywhere. You can refuse or withdraw permission in your phone's settings; the app still works, measuring distances from the middle of the map instead.

When anything leaves your phone

  • Backups you make: the app can save your visits and photos to one file, which you choose where to keep (for example Google Drive or email). Keep it private: anyone with the file can read your notes.
  • Android's own backup: if your phone's backup to your Google account is turned on, Android copies the app's visits, ratings, notes and settings (not photos) there, encrypted. This is a feature of Android, controlled in your phone's settings and covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Links you tap (tickets, websites, directions, reviews) open other websites or apps, which have their own privacy policies. The app itself sends nothing to them.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash-reporting tools inside the app, no selling or sharing of data: there is no data to share. Google Play may show us anonymous totals (such as installs and crashes) that it collects itself, under its own policies; these do not identify you.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The contact form is run by Google Forms: what you type in it (including an email address, if you give one so we can reply) is passed to us by that service, under its privacy policy, and used only to reply.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete: your records are on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them (save a backup first if you want to keep them). If you have a concern about how your data is handled, you can contact us through the contact form, or the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts to collect any data, it will ask you first.
